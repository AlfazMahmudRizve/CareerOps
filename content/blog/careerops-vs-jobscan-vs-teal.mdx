## 2. In-Depth Platform Analysis

### Jobscan.co — The Legacy Pioneer
Jobscan pioneered ATS match scoring by comparing resume text against job descriptions. 

- **Strengths**: Established brand recognition and extensive educational guides on ATS platforms like Workday and Taleo.
- **Weaknesses**: At **$49.95/month**, Jobscan is one of the most expensive job seeker tools on the market. Its free tier is heavily restricted (limiting users to 2–5 total scans), and its slow 4–8 second processing speed lags behind modern LPU engines.

### Teal HQ — The Career CRM & Application Tracker
Teal HQ positions itself as a comprehensive career growth platform centered around a browser extension and application tracker CRM.

- **Strengths**: Excellent browser integration for bookmarking job listings across LinkedIn and Indeed.
- **Weaknesses**: Tailoring resumes and generating AI bullet points requires subscribing to Teal+ ($9 to $13 per week). Additionally, Teal stores user resume data and job search histories on persistent cloud databases.

### CareerOps — The Speed & Privacy Leader
CareerOps was engineered from the ground up to eliminate subscription inflation and data retention risks for job seekers.

- **Strengths**: Powered by **Groq LPU hardware**, CareerOps delivers sub-second (under 0.5s) AI resume tailoring. It operates on a **Stateless Zero Retention** model — resume text is processed in memory and never stored or sold.
- **Weaknesses**: Focuses purely on resume tailoring and ATS gap analysis rather than full job tracking CRMs.
